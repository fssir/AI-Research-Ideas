# Human Contribution and Generative AI Assistance

## Human-supplied core idea

The human author supplied the central research idea:

> Train a lightweight predictive model jointly on the decimal expansions of multiple irrational mathematical constants and test whether any next-digit predictive structure transfers to irrational constants that are completely absent from training.

The human author also specified the use of AI tools for research assistance and directed the desired experimental outputs, figures, and manuscript structure.

## AI-assisted work

Generative AI tools assisted with:

- targeted literature organization and novelty checking;
- refinement of the low-compute experimental design;
- model selection and code drafting;
- execution of the reference pilot in a sandboxed environment;
- statistical summaries and diagnostic baselines;
- figure preparation;
- manuscript drafting and language editing;
- repository organization.

## Responsibility

AI tools are not authors. The human author is responsible for independently verifying the experiment, references, numerical claims, and final manuscript before external submission.
