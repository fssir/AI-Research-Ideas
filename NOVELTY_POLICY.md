# Novelty and Originality Policy

ORI is intended for research ideas that the contributor believes are original.

## Required standard

The contributor must:

1. originate the core idea or have the right to submit it;
2. perform a reasonable search of publicly accessible scholarly/prior-art sources;
3. disclose the search process;
4. disclose the closest known public work;
5. explain the substantive difference;
6. update the record if materially relevant prior work is later discovered.

## What ORI does not claim

ORI does not certify worldwide absolute novelty, patent novelty/patentability, peer-reviewed validity, absence of unpublished prior work, or absence of earlier work missed by the contributor's search.

“Not found in public search” means only that the contributor did not identify the same core idea through the documented search process.

A submission may be rejected, flagged, archived, or removed if clear prior disclosure is identified, originality information is materially misleading, content is plagiarized, evidence/citations are fabricated, or the submission violates law, ethics, privacy, confidentiality, or licensing requirements.
