# Originality Policy

AI Research Ideas is intended for genuinely original research ideas.

A contributor should submit an Idea only if they sincerely believe the core idea is their own and has not already been publicly disclosed in the academic literature.

The repository does not require a formal novelty-search form and does not certify worldwide absolute novelty, patentability, peer-reviewed validity, or the absence of prior work that a contributor failed to find.

If clear earlier public work is later discovered, the Idea can be updated to acknowledge it, re-scoped, or removed as appropriate.

Plagiarism, knowingly false originality claims, fabricated citations, and fabricated research results presented as real are not permitted.
