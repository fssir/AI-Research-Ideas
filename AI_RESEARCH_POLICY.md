# AI-Assisted Research Policy

ORI is explicitly designed for AI-assisted research execution.

## Human contributor

The human contributor supplies the key original idea and core research prompt, defines or approves the scientific objective, and remains accountable for what is published under their name.

## AI systems may assist with

- literature discovery and synthesis;
- coding and debugging;
- dataset preparation;
- numerical/simulation experiments;
- analysis;
- figure generation;
- manuscript drafting;
- documentation;
- repository preparation when tools and permissions permit.

## Verification requirement

AI-generated output must not be presented as verified simply because an AI produced it. Contributors should verify that cited works exist, code matches the described method, datasets have legitimate provenance, reported experiments were actually executed, figures match underlying data, numerical results are reproducible, and conclusions follow from the evidence.

Each idea's metadata must disclose AI use and, where practical, the systems/tools and roles involved.
